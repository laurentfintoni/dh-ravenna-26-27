###### Exercise 1
# **Object** first
## research as inquiry

---

<!-- _footer: Image: Unsplash. <a href="https://unsplash.com/photos/trees-and-clouds-rendered-in-pencil-sketch-GsdeXxg6JQU" target="_blank">Source</a>. -->

![bg left](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1751727193052-aa863e8a32d9?q=80&w=1339)

### Exercise

**Goal**: practice the concept of 'research as inquiry'.

**What**: Starting from one item, create a set of candidates and a possible question. 

**Time**: 15 minutes. 

**Tools**: a web browser, notes/document. 

I will do a demo and then you will split into groups or individually.

---

### Object first

1. Choose a source (from the list I gave you or your own).
2. Browse that source, **no query**, pick one item that interests you. 
3. Follow its metadata (creator, subject, collection etc.) and see how many similar/related items you can find. For example paintings by the same creator. 
4. Record the item you chose, where you found it, how many others you found, and what question they could help you answer. 

#### Example

Source: <a href="https://www.europeana.eu/it" target="_blank">Europeana</a>. 
Note: I clicked collections, and under topic i chose musical instruments. 
Item: <a href="https://www.europeana.eu/it/item/2048047/Clasate_35cf13ed40c046a29e999afb2b25db32" target="_blank">Radioreceptor AEG SUPER 68WK</a>. 
Metadata: type -> <a href="https://www.europeana.eu/it/collections/topic/3339-radio-receiver" target="_blank">radio</a>. 
Similar items: yes, many! 
Possible questions: popularity of manufacturers, countries of adoptions, design.

### Exercise

**Goal**: practice the concept of 'searching as strategic exploration'.

**What**: Starting from one word, and explore how different relations change results. 

**Time**: 15 minutes. 

**Tools**: a web browser, notes/document. 

I will do a demo and then you will split into groups or individually.

---

### Term ladder

1. Choose a word for a topic of interest (or a metadata term from exercise 1).
2. Use <a href="https://thes.bncf.firenze.sbn.it/ricerca.php" target="_blank">Nuovo soggettario</a> or <a href="https://www.getty.edu/research/tools/vocabularies/aat/" target="_blank">Getty AAT</a> to explore its ladder: broader, narrower, related, translated. Choose a few alternative terms.
3. Using your chosen word + chosen alternatives search at least 2 sources and see how the results differ. 
4. Record the chosen term, alternatives, sources, and which felt most useful for you. 

#### Example

Term: radio (from exercise 1). 
Ladder: <a href="https://thes.bncf.firenze.sbn.it/termine.php?id=28178" target="_blank">Nuovo Soggettario</a>. Alternative terms: Strumenti; Propaganda radiofonica; Teatro radiofonico.
Search: Europeana; Catalogo Beni Culturali. 
Result: Teatro radiofonico in Europeana revealed a collection for Spanish magazine called Ondas.
